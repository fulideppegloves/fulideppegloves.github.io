Buyer FAQ

Can Fulide quote OEM or private-label orders?Yes. Send product direction, target market, quantity, logo or packing notes and required documents. The factory will quote only after the scope is clear.
Are leather grade and glove protection level the same thing?No. Leather grade is a commercial material selection concept. Glove protection level comes from tests such as EN 388, ANSI/ISEA 105, EN 407 or EN 12477 when applicable.
