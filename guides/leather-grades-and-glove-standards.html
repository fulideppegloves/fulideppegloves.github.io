Leather Grades and Glove Protection Standards | Fulide Guide

A buyer guide explaining the difference between leather commercial grade and tested glove protection levels for leather safety glove sourcing.

Direct answer

Leather has commercial grades used for buying material, but there is no universal A/B/C grade that automatically proves glove protection. Protection levels come from glove tests and standards, and they must match the exact glove construction, sample and market.

TopicWhat it meansBuyer action
Leather commercial gradeSelection by hide area, defect tolerance, thickness, softness, usable area and finish.Confirm sample, feel, thickness range and acceptable defects.
EN 388 / ANSI/ISEA 105Mechanical test performance such as abrasion, cut, tear and puncture.Ask which exact test, target level, sample and report are needed.
EN 12477Welding glove requirements; Type A and Type B involve different performance tradeoffs.Match TIG/MIG/arc use, dexterity and heat exposure before quoting.
EN 407Thermal risk tests where applicable to heat contact or flame exposure.Do not use thermal claims without exact tested properties.
